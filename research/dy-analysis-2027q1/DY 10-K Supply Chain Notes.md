### Supply chain / vendor concentration — notable NEGATIVE finding
10-K explicitly states DY has **no material single-source supplier or subcontractor dependency**:
"Subcontractors and Materials — We contract with subcontractors... These subcontractors are typically small, privately owned companies... No individual subcontractor is financially significant to the Company. For a majority of the contract services we perform, we are provided most of the required materials by our customers... Under contracts that require us to supply part or all of the required materials, we typically do not depend upon any one source for those materials." (Item 1, Business)

DY is a labor/construction-services contractor, not a manufacturer — customers (telecom carriers) often supply materials (fiber, cable, equipment) themselves. No named key suppliers disclosed anywhere in the 10-K. The supply-chain risk in this business is structurally about **labor/subcontractor availability**, not vendor/material concentration.
