# FuelCell Energy, Inc. — Prior 6 Quarters Trend Data (Q1 FY2026 back through Q4 FY2024)

Compiled to support 10-quarter/multi-quarter trend analysis alongside the Q2 FY2026 10-Q. Fiscal year end October 31. All dollar figures in **thousands** except per-share data. All figures pulled directly from SEC EDGAR primary filings (fetched via https://r.jina.ai/ text-extraction proxy after direct sec.gov WebFetch repeatedly returned HTTP 403).

**Methodology note on Q4 figures:** FuelCell Energy does not file a 10-Q for its fiscal Q4 (it files a 10-K covering the full fiscal year instead). Q4 FY2025 and Q4 FY2024 figures below are **derived by subtraction**: (full fiscal year 10-K figures) − (nine-months-ended-July-31 cumulative figures from the Q3 10-Q of that fiscal year). This is standard practice but means Q4 figures were NOT independently reported as standalone quarterly figures by the company — they are computed here from two audited/reviewed source documents. EPS is **not** derived for Q4 quarters because weighted-average share counts differ quarter to quarter and are not simply subtractable; those cells are marked "not derived."

## Important caveats

1. **"Total Debt" column caveat:** For all rows above, the "Total Debt" figure is the sum of the balance sheet's "Current portion of long-term debt" line plus "Long-term debt and other liabilities" line, **as those exact line items are labeled in each filing**. The long-term line is a combined presentation (debt + other long-term liabilities) in FCEL's balance sheet format, so this column slightly overstates pure debt in each period. Only for Q2 FY2026 do we have a footnote-based "pure debt" figure ($134.2M) to compare against the combined balance-sheet-line figure ($146,901K) — implying roughly $12-13M of "other liabilities" embedded in that line for that quarter. Treat the Total Debt column here as directionally useful but not a precise standalone debt figure for quarters other than Q2 FY2026.
2. **Total revenues by category (product/service/generation/advanced technologies) were not separately re-extracted for every quarter** in this file to keep it concise — see the individual source 10-Qs/10-K for full category detail, and see "FCEL Q2 10-Q Key Data.md" for the Q2 FY2026 category breakdown side-by-side with its Q2 FY2025 comparative.
