## Form 4 insider-trading scan (2026-06 ~ 2026-08-04)

- Most recent Form 4 filings before/around the Q4 print: 2026-07-15 (Amy Coleman, EVP Chief HR Officer — code `F`, 32.344 shares withheld for tax on RSU vest, ~$12.4K, not a discretionary sale), 2026-07-01, and a cluster of routine RSU-vest tax-withholding filings dated 2026-06-09 ~ 2026-06-16 (fiscal year-end vesting wave — normal seasonal pattern).
- **No Form 4 filings at all between 2026-07-15 and 2026-08-04** — i.e. a clean blackout window through the earnings date and the week after. No open-market discretionary sales/purchases by Section 16 officers in the run-up to or aftermath of the print.
- ⚑ Read: no anomalous insider signal around this quarter's beat — the absence of activity is itself informative given how active the June RSU-vest cluster was; it's consistent with a standard pre-earnings blackout rather than a special circumstance.

- Intelligent Cloud revenue grew 29.7% YoY on an annual basis (FY26 vs FY25) — the call's 32%/43% Azure figures are Q4-specific and not directly comparable to this FY total, but the direction and magnitude are consistent (Q4 accelerated the full-year average up).

## Customer/geographic concentration (R108.htm)

- **"No sales to an individual customer or country other than the United States accounted for more than 10% of revenue for fiscal years 2026, 2025, or 2024."** — this is the 10-K's formal disclosure boundary. It does not contradict the call's "nearly 90% of cloud revenue from customers outside Frontier Model companies" framing (OpenAI is a large customer but apparently still under the 10% company-wide revenue threshold, or the disclosure is at the consolidated-revenue level rather than cloud-segment level — flagged as a scope difference, not a discrepancy).

## Confidence / scope note

Given the size of the FY2026 10-K (8.5MB main document), this cross-check focused on Form 4 insider activity, the audited segment table, PP&E, and RPO detail — the disclosures most load-bearing for verifying the call's headline claims — rather than a line-by-line re-derivation of every quarterly figure. Quarterly-level Azure/Intelligent Cloud detail (the 43%/41% growth figures) comes from the earnings call prepared remarks, which is standard for this repo's framework since MSFT does not break out Azure revenue in the 10-K itself (Azure is disclosed only as a growth-rate callout in MD&A, not a line item in the audited segment table).
