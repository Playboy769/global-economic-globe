## 6. Cobre Panamá restart status (major risk/catalyst — 2026 developments)

Source: FNV Q2 2026 MD&A (Exhibit 99.2, Acc. 0001104659-26-094181) + web search, accessed 2026-09-23.

### Timeline
- **Nov 2023**: Cobre Panamá production halted; mine placed under "preservation and safe
  management" (P&SM) following nationwide protests over the mine's environmental impact and fiscal
  terms.
- **FY2023 result**: FNV recorded a **net loss of $(466.4)M** for FY2023 (per 40-F comparative
  table, §2 above) — very likely tied to a Cobre Panamá stream impairment; **confirm exact
  impairment amount by pulling the FY2023 40-F directly** (not done in this pass).
- **May 30, 2025**: Government of Panama approved and formally instructed execution of the P&SM
  plan.
- **Sept 11, 2025**: FNV reached a settlement with the Canada Revenue Agency (unrelated tax matter,
  not Cobre Panamá, but referenced in same period — see §7).
- **Apr 7, 2026**: Panama issued a processing-program authorization: "authorized the removal,
  processing, and export of stockpiled ore" at Cobre Panamá.
- **Jun 19, 2026**: Independent environmental audit (by SGS Panama, per web search) completed,
  confirming **361 of 370 environmental commitments fulfilled — 87.7%–88% overall compliance rate**,
  per FNV's own MD&A language; external reporting (Mining Weekly, MINING.COM, June 22, 2026) puts the
  compliance score at "roughly 88%," while flagging **deficiencies in biodiversity, ecological
  restoration/reforestation, and future liabilities tied to the tailings facility, water quality,
  acid drainage, and erosion**.
- **Q2 2026 (through Jun 30, 2026)**: 2.1 million tonnes of stockpiled ore processed, producing
  3,216 tonnes of copper, under the limited stockpile-processing authorization (not a full
  operational restart).
- **2026 full-year guidance** (First Quantum, cited in FNV MD&A): 30,000–40,000 tonnes of copper
  from stockpile processing; corresponding FNV stream deliveries expected **H2 2026: ~23,100 gold
  ounces and ~265,000 silver ounces**.
- **Government decision timeline** (per Mining Weekly / The Deep Dive / Rio Times, June 2026):
  President José Raúl Mulino had committed to a **decision by June 2026** on whether to permanently
  decommission the site or negotiate a full restart under a restructured legal framework. Formal
  restart negotiations with First Quantum are reported to be expected to begin **late 2026 or early
  2027**, after completion of environmental/legal reviews. One source (Rio Times) notes Panama is
  also considering a **state-owned company** as an operating vehicle for reopening the mine.

⚑ **Distinguish clearly in the report**: what has happened (stockpile processing authorized and
underway, audit passed at ~88%) vs. what has NOT happened (no full operational restart, no final
government decision on long-term status, no restructured concession agreement with First Quantum as
of the Q2 2026 call). This is a live, unresolved catalyst/risk — the report should not overstate
certainty either direction.

## 7. Karma royalty litigation (Burkina Faso) — background for risk tab

Source: web search (Ecofin Agency, TipRanks, The Globe and Mail, thedeepdive.ca, Ghana Business
News, Canadian Mining Journal, Newswire.ca, StockTitan — cross-referenced), plus FNV's own 6-K filed
2026-06-17 (Acc. 0001104659-26-074937, Exhibit 99.1 — a company response/press release on this
matter). Accessed 2026-09-23.

- **Asset**: Karma gold mine, ~195 km from Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso's Yaadga region.
- **History**: Original gold-purchase/streaming agreement signed **August 11, 2014** by the mine's
  then-owners, **True Gold** (later acquired by **Endeavour Mining**). Franco-Nevada (and
  **Sandstorm Gold**, jointly) hold the stream. Mine was later sold (March 2022) by Endeavour to
  **Néré Mining SA**, a Burkinabè company, which now operates it as **Riverstone Karma**.
  Riverstone/Néré inherited the 2014 stream obligation from the prior owners.
- **The judgment**: On **June 10, 2026**, the **Commercial Court of Ouagadougou** ruled against
  Franco-Nevada and Sandstorm, **nullifying the 2014 gold-purchase agreement** and ordering payment
  of **CFA 5.2 billion (~US$9.3 million)** plus legal costs. The court rejected FNV's/Sandstorm's
  jurisdictional challenge and found Riverstone Karma's claims well-founded.
- **FNV's position/response**: The stream agreement is **governed by Ontario law** (per the
  contract's own dispute-resolution clause). Franco-Nevada considers the Burkina Faso judgment
  **invalid** and is seeking to have it **vacated**; it is separately pursuing its own remedies **in
  Ontario and elsewhere** under the agreement's dispute-resolution provisions against Riverstone
  Karma SA, parent **Néré Mining SA**, and other affiliates.
- Coverage notes this as part of a **"legal battle widening"** (Ghana Business News headline,
  2026-07-26), suggesting escalation/appeals are ongoing as of report-writing time.

⚑ **Not found in the Q2 2026 earnings release/MD&A excerpts pulled** — the WebFetch of Exhibit 99.1
and 99.2 for Q2 2026 did **not** surface any Karma/Burkina Faso mention in the portions extracted;
it's plausible the topic is covered elsewhere in the MD&A (e.g., a "Legal Proceedings" or
"Contingencies" note in the full financial statements, Exhibit 99.3, which was not fetched in this
pass) or was discussed live on the Aug 12, 2026 earnings call (per the user's framing: "the Karma
royalty litigation mentioned on the call") rather than in the written release. **Recommend**: (a)
pull Exhibit 99.3 (interim financial statements / notes) from the Q2 2026 6-K for a contingencies
note, and (b) if an earnings-call transcript is available (e.g., via Seeking Alpha, Motley Fool,
