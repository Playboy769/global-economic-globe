## 4. Customer Concentration (MD&A + Note 10, Fair Value of Financial Instruments)

- **Accounts receivable**: $314.0M total as of June 30, 2026, of which **$211.0M (67.2%) due from Digicomm International Inc.**
- **Revenue concentration** (MD&A "Customer Concentration"): For the six months ended June 30, 2026, revenue from **Digicomm** was approximately **$147.0 million, ~42.8% of consolidated revenue**, primarily attributable to the CATV product category.
- **Top ten customers**: represented **99%** of revenue for Q2 2026 vs. **98%** for Q2 2025.
- **Top three customers** (unnamed except Digicomm, which is clearly the largest given the $147.0M/42.8% match to the "43%" figure below):
  - Q2 2026: 42%, 26%, 24% of revenue respectively
  - Q2 2025: 54%, 34%, 6% of revenue respectively
  - 6mo 2026: 43%, 26%, 24% of revenue respectively
  - 6mo 2025: 59%, 30%, 5% of revenue respectively
- Management's stated view: "does not believe this concentration will create a material credit-risk," attributing the AR concentration to extended payment terms granted to Digicomm to help it maintain inventory for network builds; expects the concentration to decline "to the extent customers in the Company's data center business continue to grow relative to Digicomm."

⚑ Customer concentration is extremely high and narrowing only in relative (not absolute) terms — top three customers are 92% of Q2 2026 revenue combined (42+26+24), essentially unchanged in concentration from a year ago (54+34+6=94%) even though the *identity mix* has shifted toward Data Center customers.

## 6. Warrant / Contra-Revenue Disclosure (Note 3, Revenue Recognition — "Customer Warrant")

- On **March 13, 2025**, AAOI issued a warrant (the "Customer Warrant") to **a wholly-owned subsidiary of Amazon.com, Inc.** to purchase up to an aggregate of **7,945,399 shares** of common stock at an exercise price of **$23.6956/share**.
- Contractual term: **10 years**.
- At issuance, **1,324,233 Warrant Shares** were immediately exercisable; the remaining **6,621,166 Warrant Shares** vest over 10 years, contingent on **aggregate purchases by Amazon and affiliates of $4 billion of AAOI products** over that period.
- Accounting treatment: equity-classified share-based consideration to a customer; grant-date fair value recognized as a **reduction of revenue from Amazon** as goods/services are transferred (i.e., contra-revenue).
- Grant-date fair value assumptions (Black-Scholes-Merton): expected volatility 80.00%, weighted-average expected term 10 years, risk-free rate 4.23%, dividend yield 0%, fair value per share at grant date **$15.87** (Note 3) — cross-referenced elsewhere in the filing as $12.64/share for the immediately-vested tranche specifically (Note 10 language); both figures appear in the filing and are reported here as stated rather than reconciled.
- **P&L impact**: for the six months ended June 30, 2026, AAOI recognized approximately **$2.1 million reduction to $84.8 million of revenue** associated with warrant-linked customer arrangements (i.e., $84.8M of gross Amazon-related revenue before the $2.1M contra-revenue warrant charge).
- Balance sheet: other current assets of $3.7M and other noncurrent assets of $12.6M as of June 30, 2026 (vs. $1.0M current / $15.0M noncurrent at Dec 31, 2025), representing the aggregate grant-date fair value of the 1,324,233 warrant shares vested at issuance.
- Cash-flow statement "Warrants contra revenue" non-cash add-back: $2,130K (6mo 2026) vs. $53K (6mo 2025) — consistent with the $2.1M P&L figure above.
- Separately, a **non-cash financing/operating activity** line "Warrant issued and vested to customer" of $1,775K appears at period-end (six months ended June 30, 2026) — full comparative figure was cut off in extraction; see filing directly if the exact prior-year comparative is needed.
