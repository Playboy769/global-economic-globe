## Key findings used in Analysis.html / Financials.html

1. **Segment reorganization**: FY2025 10-K still shows two segments (Cloud & Networking 85.8%, Industrial Tech 14.2%). The Q3 FY26 10-Q confirms Lumentum moved to a **single reportable segment** starting Q1 FY2026 — CODM (CEO) reviews consolidated net income only. No like-for-like segment revenue split exists post-reorg; Financials.html uses the Components/Systems product-category split disclosed on the earnings call instead.

2. **"NVIDIA direct investment" (CFO's phrase) = Series A Convertible Preferred Stock**. The $2.02B cash increase the CFO attributed to "NVIDIA's direct investment" lines up almost exactly with the 10-Q's disclosure of a first-ever Series A Convertible Preferred Stock issuance (2.9M shares, ~$1,999.7M net proceeds), issued alongside a new 2032 Convertible Notes offering (~$1,254.7M net) + $102.0M capped call, and a partial repurchase of 2026 Notes ($843.1M cash + $256.9M equity inducement charge + $5.9M cash inducement). No analyst on the call asked about pricing, conversion terms, lock-up, or board rights.

3. **Customer concentration jumped intra-year**: 10-K FY25 annual: Customer A 16.0%, Customer B 15.4%. 10-Q Q3 FY26 (3-month): 26% + 12% = 38%, vs. Q3 FY25 comp of 17%+15%=32%. Neither filing names customers; transcript repeatedly names Google as "one of our largest customers" but this cannot be directly confirmed as the SEC-anonymized "Customer A."

4. **Contract-manufacturer concentration improved**: 10-K FY25 = 25.1% of inventory purchases from one CM; FY24 = 30.3%. 10-Q Q3 FY26 (3-month and 9-month) = 19%, down from 27% in the FY25 comp periods.

5. **Huawei / BIS / DOJ**: 10-K discloses Lumentum stopped all shipments to Huawei (its historically largest China networking customer) in early 2024 per EAR export controls, and discloses an active BIS administrative subpoena (Aug 2024) and a related DOJ subpoena, both unresolved as of the 10-K date. Not mentioned anywhere in the earnings call transcript.

6. **China critical-minerals risk factor** names gallium and germanium export restrictions specifically — directly relevant to Lumentum's InP/GaAs laser-chip inputs. Not mentioned in the transcript.

7. **Manufacturing footprint** (10-K Item 1): own fabs in US, Thailand, China, UK, Slovenia, Japan; CM partners in Thailand, Taiwan, Malaysia, Philippines. PP&E by country (10-Q note 17, as of 2026-03-28): Thailand $218.6M (largest), Japan $144.3M, US $123.0M, UK $109.4M, China $76.8M, other $54.3M (total $726.4M). The transcript's "fifth indium phosphide fab in Greensboro, NC" is **not corroborated** in either filing — flagged as transcript-only throughout both HTML reports.

8. **Form 4 window (~Mar–May 2026)**: no insider purchases at all; a coordinated tax-withholding vesting event across CEO/CFO/GC/BU president on 2026-05-15 at $970.70, followed by open-market sales from all of them **except the CEO** on 2026-05-18 at $953.95; three independent directors (Small, Lillie, Fletcher) sold blocks between 2026-05-08 and 2026-05-22; transaction prices drifted from ~$1,004 (May 11-14) down to ~$868 (May 21), an observed ~13-14% decline usable as a real, SEC-sourced share-price data point (used in the valuation tab's rough P/E estimate).
