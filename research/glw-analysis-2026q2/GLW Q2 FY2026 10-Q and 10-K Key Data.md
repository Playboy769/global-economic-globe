## NVIDIA Warrant / Long-Term Supply Agreement (May 6, 2026) — Special Transaction

- **Counterparty confirmed as NVIDIA** via the 10-Q's XBRL tagging (`glw:NVIDIAMember` cross-referenced with `glw:PurchaseAgreementMember` for the six months ended June 30, 2026) — the narrative text of Note 12 only says "a customer to strengthen U.S. manufacturing for AI infrastructure," but the machine-readable XBRL member tag names NVIDIA explicitly. Timing (May 6, 2026) matches the transcript's own account: "in May, NVIDIA announced a multi-year commercial and technology partnership with Corning to dramatically expand U.S.-based manufacturing of the advanced optical connectivity solutions."
- Structure: securities purchase agreement issuing (i) a **Traditional Warrant** to purchase up to **15 million shares** at a **$180.00/share** exercise price, and (ii) a **Pre-Funded Warrant** to purchase up to **3 million shares** at a nominal **$0.0001/share** exercise price — **18 million warrant shares combined**
- Traditional Warrant: issued for no separate cash consideration; because it conveys no distinct good/service to Corning, it is accounted for as **consideration payable to the customer** — grant-date fair value **$296M** (binomial lattice, Level 3 inputs), recorded in additional paid-in capital, and will be recognized as a **reduction of revenue** as sales occur under the agreement (i.e., it directly dilutes the economics of the $1.0B deposit over the contract's life through 2029). Not equity-classified for remeasurement purposes — measured once at grant date only.
- Pre-Funded Warrant: issued for **$500 million cash**, approximating fair value at issuance; recorded in additional paid-in capital; because of the nominal exercise price, the underlying 3 million shares are treated as **already outstanding for diluted EPS purposes** (already reflected in the 875M Q2 diluted share count)
- Both warrants: equity-classified, exercisable any time from issuance; Traditional Warrant expires at the earliest of (a) 3rd anniversary of issuance, (b) termination of the underlying definitive partnership agreement, (c) a Corning fundamental transaction (e.g. change of control)
- Linked contract terms: the **$1.0 billion customer deposit** secures rights under a long-term supply agreement running **through December 31, 2029**; net of the $296M warrant consideration, Corning recorded a ~**$0.7 billion net contract liability addition** in Q2 2026
- Cash impact: the $500M Pre-Funded Warrant proceeds hit financing activities; the $1.0B deposit (net of item timing) is the primary driver cited by the company for the doubling of operating cash flow in 1H26 vs 1H25
- Cross-reference for 互證 (dual corroboration): management's public narrative ("NVIDIA... to dramatically expand U.S.-based manufacturing") is corroborated quantitatively by (a) the $1.0B customer deposit recognized in contract liabilities, (b) the $500M warrant cash proceeds in financing activities, (c) the operating cash flow doubling explicitly attributed by the company to this deposit, and (d) the capex guide raise from ~$1.7B to ~$2.0B in the same quarter the deposit was received — a textbook case of a customer-funded capacity-expansion deal showing up simultaneously across four separate financial statement lines.
