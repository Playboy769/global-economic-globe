⚠️ **These DSO figures are materially distorted and should not be read as a straightforward working-capital deterioration signal.** The 10-Q's own MD&A explicitly warns: *"We continue to be impacted by netted costs that we apply to our services net sales to appropriately record net sales that we earn as an agent... calculation of our unadjusted cash conversion cycle, including days sales outstanding and days payables outstanding, do not provide an accurate reflection of our cash conversion metric, due to the metric components being inherently inflated. For example, netted costs were $5.1 billion and $3.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and 2025, respectively."* In plain terms: AR/AP carry the **gross** amount Insight collects/pays as principal on agency (net-revenue) transactions, even though only the net margin shows up in net sales — so DSO computed against net sales overstates receivable days by a wide and *growing* margin (netted costs grew from $3.6B to $5.1B YoY, roughly matching the DSO climb from ~238 to ~296 days). The rising trend across all 6 quarters is consistent with Insight's growing agency/cloud mix (more net-basis transactions), not necessarily worsening collections. Long-term AR ($699M, separately reported) is excluded from this calc; including it would push DSO higher still.

**(b) Leases not yet commenced** — 無此資料/不適用. The Q2 FY26 10-Q contains no dedicated "Leases" note and no disclosure of leases signed but not yet commenced (ASC 842). The only lease-adjacent language is boilerplate risk-factor text ("we will be able to extend our current leases or locate substitute facilities on satisfactory terms") and the debt-note title "Debt, Inventory Financing Facilities, **Finance Leases** and Other Financing Obligations" — but "Other financing obligations" (which would include finance leases) shows $0 at Jun 30, 2026 and only $41K at Dec 31, 2025, i.e., immaterial. No future-lease-commencement disclosure exists to report.

**(c) Purchase commitments by maturity** — two committed cloud/SaaS purchase agreements disclosed in Note 8 (Commitments and Contingencies), not broken out by year-bucket (Insight discloses total/remaining only, not a maturity ladder like JNJ-style tables):
- Cloud services agreement: minimum required purchase commitment ≈$100,467K over a 5-year period (Oct 1, 2023 – Sep 30, 2028); **remaining commitment at Jun 30, 2026 = $47,274,000**. If the total isn't met by Sep 30, 2028, the shortfall must be prepaid (usable through Sep 30, 2029).
- SaaS agreement: minimum required purchase commitment ≈$40,000,000 over a 4-year period (Nov 30, 2022 – Nov 29, 2026); **as of Jun 30, 2026, the minimum has been satisfied — no remaining commitment outstanding**.
- Separately, a **contingent liability of ~$28,826,000** is recorded, payable to a partner to settle contractual commitments to resell a minimum amount of cloud services to clients.
- Performance bonds outstanding: ~$38,337,000 (not accrued as a liability; payment not deemed probable).
